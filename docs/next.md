# What I'd do next

Ideas decided against for this build, kept for later. Each says what would make it worth doing. These feed the readout's "What I'd ship next".

## Book first, add the card later (B4b)

- **Idea:** book now and add the card 24 to 72 hours before the visit, or the booking is released
- **Precedent:** Rula lets patients reserve a slot first and add a card during registration. If registration isn't finished within six days of booking or 14 hours before the appointment, whichever is first, the appointment is cancelled (`docs/research.md`, questions 3 and 5)
- **Why not now:** there's no evidence payment is where people drop. Every session that creates an account reaches the payment page (1,403 of 1,403), and nothing after that is measured. A card at booking protects against no-shows
- **When to test it:** once payment success is tracked. If many patients drop at the card step, test reserve-first against card at booking, 50/50
- **Guardrail:** no-show rate

## Email sign-in link to come back (from A1)

- **Idea:** an emailed link so patients can pick up where they left off on any device
- **Why not now:** it needs an email service. For now, progress saves on the same device
- **When:** after the pre-visit intake works end to end

## Standard screeners in the check-in (suggestion for Legion's clinical team)

- **Idea:** offer standard screeners like the PHQ-9 and GAD-7 as part of the pre-visit check-in
- **Why not now:** which screeners to use, and when, is a clinical decision for Legion's team. The prototype suggests it but doesn't build it
- **When:** if Legion's clinical team wants them

## Worried about someone else

- **Idea:** a path for people who come to the flow for someone else, like a partner or a parent. Today the prototype flags it and explains they'll need to sign up themselves (golden-set case W-14)
- **Why not now:** helping a family member get care raises consent and privacy questions for Legion's clinical and legal teams. Common in immigrant families, where one person often handles the paperwork
- **When:** if Legion's teams want to support family members helping someone sign up
