  High

  1. "Leave this page" leaves the patient's words one Back away (seen if tested;
     main and #23). help-now/being-hurt/LeaveThisPage.tsx:14 replaces only that
     one page in the browser history. The page before it is "What's going on?",

     from Google shows what the person wrote about being hurt.
  2. Arrow keys on Yes/No questions trigger screen-outs and the crisis page 
     (seen; main and #23). In components/SegmentedControl.tsx:43, choosing an
     option immediately leaves the page. Keyboard users who arrow to "No" on
     "Are you in Texas?" are screened out, and arrowing to "Yes" on the safety
     question opens the crisis page. It also records a screen-out in the
     database and in PostHog, which skews the screen-out rate.
  3. Anyone can read and overwrite another person's details with just their 
     email (code; main and #23). api/join/sign-in/route.ts:23-25 links any
     existing person to the current visitor.
     - The profile page then shows that person's name, email and phone.
     - If the visitor edits their details again, details/route.ts:34 overwrites
       that person's record.
     - It also merges the two people in PostHog.
  4. Consent is given before the notice is shown (seen; PR #27 says so itself).
     In #23 the consent checkbox is on details (DetailsForm.tsx:186) and the
     terms notice is on payment (PaymentForm.tsx:147). consented_at is saved on
     details, which contradicts merged decision #24.
  5. The README on main is still a draft (seen).
     - The [fill in] placeholders remain for Live, Repo, PRs, Walkthrough, "What
       I built" and Demo controls.
     - The brief's required "Inspo Book 01" credit line and the photo credits
       are missing.
     - It says the checkout is "on one page", but the brief asks for two steps.
  6. A placeholder patient quote shows on every desktop screen in #23 (seen).
     "[A short quote from a real Legion patient] / Placeholder: Legion to
     supply" comes from copy.ts:53-54 and is shown at PathPanel.tsx:113-116.
  7. The tracking can't measure the one metric (seen by a data reviewer).
     - analysis/metrics.md:10 divides first visits by people who booked. The
       brief divides by everyone who starts, minus screen-outs.
     - The #25 PostHog funnel doesn't take screen-outs out.
     - portal.fit.submitted never fires for tap screen-outs, so it's always
       "eligible".
     - People who use "Sign in to continue" never fire userCreated
       (DetailsForm.tsx:71), so they drop out of the funnel even if they finish
       their visit.

  Medium

  8. Health data goes to PostHog on an identified person. The concern labels and

  9. Phone sheets have no Close button a screen reader can reach (#23).

  16. The demo controls can count a completed visit twice. Clicking completed,

  18. The insurer logos in #26 contradict the brief.

  19. The terms notice has no links. It names a "HIPAA Notice of Privacy
